# Payment logo sources

Original brand colors and proportions are retained. Logos appear in disabled,
explicitly labelled previews; they do not indicate live payment acceptance.
The gateway integration must filter the method/provider metadata to its supported
options before enabling checkout.

| Local asset | Source |
| --- | --- |
| google-pay.svg | [Google Pay brand assets](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines), Google-Pay-Acceptance.zip, Google Pay Mark/google-pay-mark_800.svg |
| phonepe.svg | [PhonePe wordmark on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PhonePe_Logo.svg), sourced from phonepe.com |
| paytm.svg | [Paytm website logo](https://pwebassets.paytm.com/commonwebassets/paytmweb/header/images/logo.svg) |
| visa.png | [Visa official CDN](https://cdn.visa.com/v2/assets/images/logos/visa/blue/logo.png), linked from visa.co.in organization metadata |
| mastercard.svg | [Mastercard logo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mastercard_2019_logo.svg), sourced from Mastercard brand guidelines |
| rupay.svg | [RuPay logo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RuPay.svg), sourced from NPCI brand guidelines |

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. No logos were redrawn
or generated. SVGs are served as images, rather than inserted into the page DOM.
